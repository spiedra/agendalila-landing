# AgendaLila — marketing landing

<!-- Maintainers: what belongs in this file is defined in .claude/rules/documentation.md § Documents. -->

The marketing site for **AgendaLila**, a booking and CRM SaaS, aimed at beauty-business
**owners** (salons, barbershops, manicuristas, spas, estética), never their clients.
Invitation-only: the site's one goal is an owner requesting an invitation. AgendaLila is
built by an esthetician who runs a real studio (Estefanía Sánchez, Amorelila Estética),
and that authenticity is the pitch.

Not this repo: amorelila.com (the salon's own site), app.agendalila.com (the admin app),
`<slug>.agendalila.com` (booking pages).
