# Impeccable — AgendaLila marketing landing

> Persistent design context for this repo. Derived from the Claude Design handoff
> brief (`claude-design-setup.md` Bloque A/B), the design system
> (`colors_and_type.css`), the `marketing-saas` kit, and the design audit
> (`docs/2026-05-26-marketing-saas-design-audit.md`). This guides ALL design/UI
> decisions here. Anti-slop is non-negotiable.

## Design Context

### Users

Business **owners** of beauty / personal-care services — salons, **barbershops**,
nail bars, spas, aesthetics studios, solo estheticians, tattoo/piercing studios.
**NOT end clients.** Medium tech comfort (Instagram-savvy, not power users).
Today they juggle WhatsApp + a paper notebook + Excel; they lose appointments,
double-book, forget client preferences, and eat no-shows. When they land here
they're evaluating whether this software "gets" how they actually work.
Job-to-be-done: decide in ~60 seconds that AgendaLila understands their business
and is worth requesting an invitation.

### Brand Personality

Three words: **Premium · Intentional · Warm.** "If Apple designed salon software."
Voice: Spanish, Costa Rican **voseo** (solicitá, mirá, contanos) — human, direct,
never corporate-SaaS. The #1 brand asset is **authenticity**: "made by an
esthetician who lives the problem," not by a software house. Copy is
operator-facing and **gender-neutral** (tu agenda, tu equipo, personas);
"clienta" appears only inside product-mock copy. Emotions to evoke: trust,
recognition ("this gets me"), calm competence — never hype.

### Aesthetic Direction

Editorial, **not** dashboard-y. **Lilac as a balanced SIGNATURE, not a flood:**
warm off-white base (`#faf6fc`), deep purple text/interactive (`#725f94`), lilac
accent (`#ae96d0`) only as punctual decoration. Type: iCiel Cadena (display) +
Montserrat (body). **Light mode only.** Soft rounded corners (radius 14/20/28),
lilac-tinted shadows. Asymmetric, left-aligned, `clamp()`-driven rhythm — never
uniform. Must read fine for a **barbershop** (not exclusively feminine).

- **NO** Liquid Glass (admin-only). **NO** butterfly motif (salon-only). **NO**
  icon mark — typographic "AgendaLila" wordmark in iCiel Cadena.
- **Anti-references / the "AI look" to avoid:** purple→blue or cyan-on-dark
  gradients; gradient text on titles/metrics; decorative glassmorphism
  everywhere; nested cards; grids of identical icon+title+text cards; centering
  everything; pure black/white; Inter/Roboto/Open Sans/system for display;
  generic SaaS (AgendaPro/Fresha vibe); stock imagery; invented "500+ clientes"
  badges.

### Design Principles

1. **Authenticity over features.** Lead with the founder's story + the operator's
   real pain, not a feature checklist.
2. **Lilac signs, deep purple works.** `#ae96d0` decorates only; `#725f94` is
   every interactive/text element (WCAG AA — `#ae96d0` on white fails, never use
   it for buttons/links).
3. **Editorial rhythm, never a template.** Asymmetric zig-zag, `clamp()` spacing,
   left-aligned; no identical-card grids, nothing centered by default.
4. **Barbershop-safe by default.** Neutral voice + imagery; must sell to a barber
   as well as a salon.
5. **Invitation-only, no friction theater.** Every CTA = "Solicitá tu
   invitación"; no pricing table, no "start free." Exclusivity reads as trust.
6. **Accessible + motion-respectful.** WCAG AA contrast, ≥44px touch targets,
   `:focus-visible`, `prefers-reduced-motion`; motion only via transform/opacity,
   ease-out, no bounce.
7. **Pass the "did an AI make this?" test.** If the answer is yes, redo it.
